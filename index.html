Why I built this

Hi, I’m Chooky.

I’ve said “I’ll start Monday” more times than I can count.

New diet? Monday. Drink more water? Monday. Get back into running? You guessed it.

The problem is that Monday always turns into next Monday. 😂

What finally worked for me wasn’t a big dramatic restart. It was small stuff, done most days. A glass of water. A walk. A stretch before bed. Ticking it off and watching the streak grow.

Those small steps got me to the start line of marathons, to more than 200 parkruns, and into a life I’m actively chasing.

I made a promise to chase life, and I’m keeping it, one small habit at a time.

So I built the app I wanted. It’s calm, private, and doesn’t yell at you when you miss a day.

You don’t need a Monday to start.
You just need today.

Chooky xx Founder, Not Another Monday
