Getting started

Not Another Monday is a habit-tracking app built around consistency. It brings daily habits, water, steps, protein, fasting, journaling, weight and progress-photo tracking, event countdowns and accountability partners together in one place.

No. Connecting Apple HealthKit or Health Connect is optional. Steps, weight and hydration can always be entered manually.

You can invite an accountability partner from your Profile. Once they accept, they can see your overall streak and public stats and can send or receive short encouragement nudges.

Accountability partners cannot see your journal entries, progress photos, weight or other private personal information.

Free accounts can have 1 accountability partner. Premium accounts can add more.

On Android, long-press your home screen, choose Add widget, and find Not Another Monday. The widget can show your streak, today’s water and steps, and your next event countdown.

The home screen widget is currently available on Android only. An iOS widget is not currently available.

Streaks & habits

Your overall streak is the length of your longest unbroken run of fully completed days.

If you leave any habit incomplete for a full day, your streak resets the next time you open the app, unless an available streak freeze is applied.

Premium accounts receive up to 2 streak freezes per calendar month. If you miss a day and have a streak freeze available, it’s applied automatically and your streak is preserved. You’ll see a short notice in the app when this happens.

Free accounts don’t include streak freezes, so a missed day resets the streak.

New subscribers receive a 7-day free trial. The subscription automatically converts to a paid subscription unless it’s cancelled at least 24 hours before the trial ends.

Current subscription pricing is displayed in the app before you subscribe and is determined through the Apple App Store or Google Play.

Subscriptions are managed through the store you used to subscribe. Cancelling isn’t completed from within Not Another Monday itself.

  • iPhone: Manage App Store subscriptions, or open Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Android: Manage Google Play subscriptions, or open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions.

If you already have a subscription and move to a new device, use Restore purchases on the upgrade screen to restore access.

Refund requests are handled by Apple or Google in line with their own policies, not by us directly. You can request one through Apple’s Report a Problem page or through your Google Play order history.

Troubleshooting

First, check that your device has granted Not Another Monday the required Apple HealthKit or Health Connect permissions, and that your device has an internet connection.

If the problem continues, try signing out and signing back into the app.

Check that notifications are enabled for Not Another Monday in your device settings and that your device has an internet connection.

If notifications still aren’t arriving, try signing out and signing back into the app. If you continue to have problems, email hello@chookychasinglife.com.
